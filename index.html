// ABOUT

every good sprite starts with a bad first draft.

pix3lware started in a spare bedroom with a Game Boy, a graph-paper notebook, and way too much patience for 16×16 grids. Years later, that same patience goes into every tileset, every walk-cycle, every 4-color palette we ship.

We work the way the format demands: constraint first. No layer you don't need, no pixel you can't justify.
